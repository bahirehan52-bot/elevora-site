Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elevora (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we may receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, business information, and project requirements. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and discuss requested services.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate usage data, and server logs. The information collected depends on the services in use and their settings.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies if we enable analytics or advertising services. If Google AdSense is added, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalise ads as permitted by applicable settings and law. Visitors can learn about Google's advertising practices and controls in Google's Ads Settings and privacy information.

At the time this policy is published, this wording does not mean that Google AdSense or analytics is already installed on the website. We will update this policy if our data practices change.

  • To meet legal obligations where applicable.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website or communication channels, or disclosed when required by law. Those providers may handle information under their own privacy terms.

External links

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, including WhatsApp and email providers. We do not control their privacy practices. Review the relevant provider's policy before sharing information with them.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential project, maintain records, or meet legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not specifically for children. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.
