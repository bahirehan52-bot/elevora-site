Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Elevora ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit elevora-site.pages.dev or contact us about our services.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, you may provide details such as your name, phone number, email address, business information and project requirements. We use the information you send to respond to your enquiry and discuss the service you requested.

Contact form and WhatsApp

The contact form does not submit information to a website database or server operated by Elevora. Its JavaScript prepares a message and opens WhatsApp with the details you entered. You must review and send that message yourself. If you send it, WhatsApp/Meta processes the message and related account information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp.

If you email us or send a WhatsApp message, the information is held in the relevant email or messaging service and in our communication records. This website's form itself does not store a copy of your submission.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. When your browser requests a page, Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address, request details, browser or device information, and security or diagnostic logs to deliver, protect and maintain the website. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request information. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and Google advertising

The Google AdSense advertising tag is currently included on the homepage. The presence of this tag does not by itself mean that an advertisement is visibly displayed on every visit. When Google advertising technologies operate on a page, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to deliver, measure and personalise advertising, including ads based on a user's visits to this website or other websites, subject to applicable settings and requirements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies in your browser or use similar technologies when ads are served or measured. Information processed may include browser identifiers, IP address, device information, ad interactions and page activity. Read How AdSense uses cookies.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about choices for some participating third-party advertising companies at aboutads.info. Browser settings may let you block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies may affect some advertising or website functionality.

For information about how Google uses information on websites and apps that use its services, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Analytics

We did not identify a separate Google Analytics tracking tag in the website files reviewed for this policy. Hosting and security logs may still be processed by the hosting provider as described above. This section will be updated if we add an analytics service.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve the website.
  • To understand and manage advertising where Google advertising services are used.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and address misuse or security issues.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, advertising, fonts, email or messaging, including Cloudflare, Google and WhatsApp/Meta, as described in this policy and their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and website security.

External websites and demo projects

This website links to external websites, including live website demonstrations, WhatsApp and other third-party services. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active project, maintain business records or meet legal requirements. Technical logs are handled according to the hosting provider's retention practices. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and general audiences and are not directed specifically at children. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

Your choices and contacting us

You may choose not to contact us or provide optional information. You can manage cookies through your browser and manage Google personalised advertising through the settings linked above.
